## Agriculture and optimization

- [agricultural_nsgaII](https://github.com/eitt/agricultural_nsgaII) — Agricultural production planning with SCIP, NSGA-II, local refinement, surrogate guidance and frozen benchmark instances.
- [agri-production-decisions](https://github.com/eitt/agri-production-decisions) — Agricultural planning benchmarks comparing exact optimization, NSGA-II and surrogate methods with idle-land maintenance; execution requires the input package specified in the README.
- [TSO](https://github.com/eitt/TSO) — Prototype notebooks for two-stage cocoa irrigation optimization, irrigation costs and rainfall scenario reduction.
- [cocoa](https://github.com/eitt/cocoa) — Streamlit prototype for NASA POWER weather acquisition and cocoa biomass and water-stress modeling.
- [NSGAII---Produccion-agricola](https://github.com/eitt/NSGAII---Produccion-agricola) — Earlier MATLAB implementation of NSGA-II for multi-objective agricultural production planning and scheduling.
- [Modelo-de-optimizacion-para-la-programacion-de-la-produccion-agricola](https://github.com/eitt/Modelo-de-optimizacion-para-la-programacion-de-la-produccion-agricola) — Earlier MATLAB mixed-integer models for agricultural production planning with revenue and financial-risk objectives.
- [Rendimientos_Agricolas](https://github.com/eitt/Rendimientos_Agricolas) — Earlier R notebooks and datasets for agricultural yield preprocessing and missing-data imputation in Santander, Colombia.

## Machine learning and statistical research

- [measurement-and-control](https://github.com/eitt/measurement-and-control) — NASA C-MAPSS remaining useful life prediction with PyTorch neural networks, PSO architecture search and MILP pruning experiments.
- [2026-MAP8_IRI_Empat-a](https://github.com/eitt/2026-MAP8_IRI_Empat-a) — MAP-8 analysis of IRI empathy data using psychometrics, structural equation models, fsQCA and clustering.
- [TOBIT](https://github.com/eitt/TOBIT) — R analysis pipeline for longitudinal moral judgments, empathy and group affiliation using two-sided Tobit models.
- [EnfermerIA](https://github.com/eitt/EnfermerIA) — R psychometric analyses and a Streamlit explorer for AI attitudes and literacy, with confirmatory factor analysis and sensitivity checks.

## Economics and sustainability

- [farm_models](https://github.com/eitt/farm_models) — EU-27 agricultural sustainability analysis using entropy weighting, composite indicators and hierarchical clustering.
- [cocoa_volatility](https://github.com/eitt/cocoa_volatility) — Cocoa price volatility and transmission across Colombian and European markets, with climate and supply-chain vulnerability analysis.
- [EMH](https://github.com/eitt/EMH) — Research on Latin American equity-market predictability using conditional diffusion models, forecast benchmarks and explainable AI.
- [Teuken-Markov](https://github.com/eitt/Teuken-Markov) — Retrospective analysis of ideological transitions in the Americas using Markov benchmarks, political-economic data and geographic networks.
- [Green-Integration-by-Contradiction](https://github.com/eitt/Green-Integration-by-Contradiction) — Research materials on EU trade policy, sustainability and environmental justice, including chapter mind maps and LNG conflict data.
- [spc-coauthor-review](https://github.com/eitt/spc-coauthor-review) — AGRIBALYSE food-comparison data, retained AI outputs and interactive coauthor review materials, with human scientific review pending.
- [Pronosticos-de-precios-agricolas](https://github.com/eitt/Pronosticos-de-precios-agricolas) — Earlier R notebooks for ARIMA and GARCH forecasting of agricultural wholesale prices in Bucaramanga, Colombia.

## Decision support and scheduling

- [Weighted_Heuristics](https://github.com/eitt/Weighted_Heuristics) — AHP-weighted usability heuristics with pairwise-comparison examples and simulation notebooks.
- [Fuzzy](https://github.com/eitt/Fuzzy) — Genetic algorithm scheduling for emergency department resource allocation with fuzzy task durations and scenario visualization.
- [MOFGPM](https://github.com/eitt/MOFGPM) — Multi-objective fuzzy goal programming for emergency department resource planning, with genetic algorithms and FlexSim comparison workflows.
- [banquillo](https://github.com/eitt/banquillo) — Staff scheduling and workload allocation with a genetic algorithm, a Streamlit dashboard and spreadsheet import/export.
