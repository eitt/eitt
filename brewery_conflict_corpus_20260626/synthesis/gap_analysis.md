# Gap analysis

- More non-English searches are needed, especially Spanish for Mexico/Latin America and Hindi/regional-language coverage for India.
- Scopus or institutional database exports would improve scholarly coverage and deduplication.
- Legal dockets and agency inspection records are needed to verify allegations and outcomes.
- Community-authored sources are underrepresented in this rapid corpus.
- Quantitative environmental monitoring data are limited; wastewater and water-use mechanisms are often inferred from policy/guidance rather than case-specific measurements.

## Additional multilingual gaps

- French and German searches still need deeper source-specific work because initial results returned mostly general nightlife/noise law or non-brewery material.
- Spanish and Portuguese coverage is stronger for Latin America, but legal dockets, municipal records, and environmental agency files remain needed for confirmation.
- Additional community-authored Spanish and Portuguese sources should be collected where permission and access allow.

## Remaining language gaps after expansion

- French, German, and Italian records are still more contextual than case-specific; targeted local newspaper and court-record searching is needed for craft-brewery nuisance, wastewater, and siting disputes.
- Dutch-language coverage is stronger for noise and wastewater regulation, but Belgian and Dutch municipal enforcement files should be added where accessible.
- Additional Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Italian, and Dutch community-authored sources should be prioritized before making broader claims.
